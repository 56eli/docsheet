## 4. Options for the owner (D6)

**D6a — Part-as-work (recommended).** `work_id` stays at the lecture-title
level; `series` keeps grouping parts into programs. The 55 uncovered
part-groups (~160 rows) receive per-title works generated by **exact**
normalized-title matching (no substring fuzz — the matcher that caused C1).
- ✅ An audiobook edition is the audio of *one* lecture; per-part works map
  editions exactly (this is what the 11 lecture-part audiobook rows already do).
- ✅ Matches the evidence-granularity rule (item-level when proven).
- ⚠️ A program-level book edition (e.g. book 294 *Transcending the Levels of
  Consciousness* vs its 24-part program) has no natural part-work home — it
  stays its own work (already the case; the program relationship can later be
  expressed via a `same_material_edition`-style link if desired).

**D6b — Series-as-work for program-series.** Each of the 10 program-series
becomes ONE work (66 titles → 10 works); lanes stay per-title.
- ✅ The program's book edition joins its program work (e.g. 294 joins its
  24-part program).
- ⚠️ Per-part audiobook editions become ambiguous: the audiobook of *Advaita*
  would be an "edition of The Way to God" although it covers 1 of 12 parts —
  work identity would lose part granularity.
- ⚠️ 24-member works are unwieldy, and the 11 already-minted audiobook
  edition rows would need re-mapping to their program works.

**D6c — Hybrid.** Only program-series that HAVE a book/audio edition become
series-works; the rest stay per-part.
- ⚠️ Inconsistent semantics (identity depends on accident of which editions
  exist); not recommended.

**Recommendation: D6a + correction C1**, keeping w-power-vs-force as already
ruled (book + its Volume I part; the other 12 Volume Series parts get their
own per-volume works).
